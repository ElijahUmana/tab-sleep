      How frozen pages work

      Tab Sleep captures the entire scrollable page, including significant internal scroll regions used by web apps. The frozen result remains scrollable and local to this Chrome profile. Selecting the tab never reloads the site; click anywhere on the frozen page, or press Enter or Space, to wake it in the same tab.
